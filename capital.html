Areas of focus

Real Estate

Location and market comparisons, due diligence checklists, rental and yield analysis, and decision-support information. Property is more than the quoted price: connectivity, rental demand, vacancy, taxes, financing and exit options all matter.

What you can expect: clear evaluation of these factors. Brokerage or transaction services are offered only where required registrations are in place.

Capital Markets

Market information, research-led analysis and tools to help you understand market behaviour, instruments and risk factors.

What you can expect: educational research and analysis. Regulated research or advisory services are offered only with applicable SEBI registration.

Forex & Crypto Markets

Market education, data-led analysis and technology support. Leverage, volatility, liquidity, counterparty risk and changing regulations can materially affect outcomes.

What you can expect: information within applicable law. We do not promise profits, and you should verify any platform’s legal status, terms and fees yourself.

Long-term Investment

Research frameworks covering goals, time horizon, asset allocation concepts, risk tolerance and costs.

What you can expect: educational and research content. Personalised investment advice requires the relevant authorisation.

Short-term Trading

Market analysis, trading education, risk-management frameworks and tools for reviewing strategies. Short-term trading carries a high risk of loss and is not suitable for everyone.

What you can expect: strategy research, education and risk tools, subject to applicable regulations.

Algo & Automation

Rule-based trading tools, alerts, dashboards, integrations and workflow automation, tested for data quality, execution behaviour, slippage, downtime and failure conditions.

What you can expect: requirements document, rule specification, prototype, test report, deployment support and monitoring checklist. Live execution is subject to platform permissions and law.
