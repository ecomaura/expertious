Services

Digital Marketing

Plan and manage digital campaigns around your business goals: channel planning, campaign setup, audience targeting, creative coordination, lead-generation campaigns and performance reviews. The mix depends on your audience, budget and sales process.

What you can expect: a campaign plan, defined objectives, an agreed channel mix, regular performance updates and recommendations for improvement.

Search Engine Optimisation (SEO)

Improve how your website is understood and discovered in organic search through technical checks, site structure, keyword research, on-page optimisation, content planning, local SEO and ethical authority-building.

What you can expect: a website and search review, priority fixes, keyword-led content direction and reporting on relevant visibility and engagement.

AIO: AI Optimisation

Prepare your content and business information for AI-assisted discovery with clear information architecture, useful content, structured data where appropriate and consistent business details.

What you can expect: an assessment of content clarity, entity information and technical readiness.

GEO: Generative Engine Optimisation

Improve the chances that your brand and expertise are understood and referenced in generative search, through topic coverage, credible source material, clear answers and entity consistency.

What you can expect: a content and visibility review, topic recommendations and a practical plan.

AEO: Answer Engine Optimisation

Structure content so it answers real customer questions clearly: FAQ planning, concise explanations, schema markup where suitable and question-led pages.

What you can expect: a question and intent map, content recommendations and page-level improvements.

What our SEO work may include

  • SEO audit: indexing, crawlability, structure, page experience and technical issues
  • Keyword research based on intent, competition and business value
  • On-page work: titles, headings, internal links and content structure
  • Content planning for service pages, articles and FAQs
  • Local SEO where relevant
  • Measurement of visibility, qualified traffic and enquiries
